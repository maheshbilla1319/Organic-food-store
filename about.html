WHERE IT STARTED

From a small farm to your table.

GreenHarvest began with a small family farm and a commitment to grow food in a more thoughtful way.

Over the years, our network has grown, but our philosophy has remained simple: work with nature, respect farmers and deliver food we are proud to serve our own families.
